Privacy Policy

Merit Badge Checklist for Scoutbook

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how the Chrome extension Merit Badge Checklist for Scoutbook handles information.

No data collected, transmitted, or sold

The extension does not collect personal data. It does not transmit data to the developer or to anyone else, and it does not sell data.

Local storage

Everything the extension stores is kept locally in your browser with chrome.storage. That information stays on your device.

scouting.org session

The extension only reads the user’s existing scouting.org session so it can show that user’s scout’s merit badges. It uses the session already present in the browser for that display and does not send it elsewhere.

Affiliation

Merit Badge Checklist for Scoutbook is an independent project. It is not affiliated with Scouting America.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to furytechdevelopment@gmail.com.
